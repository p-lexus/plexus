Plexus — logo drafts

Second round. The first set drifted onto known marks — a four-way asterisk is Claude's, and two facing arcs read as the App Store bag. These four deliberately avoid the shapes AI and network projects already occupy. Judge them at 16 px in solid black first: a mark that survives that is a logo, one that needs colour and size to work is decoration.

01Hop

The schematic convention for wires that cross without connecting. Two agents sharing a broker, never touching — the concept stated in the vocabulary of engineering drawings rather than metaphor.
